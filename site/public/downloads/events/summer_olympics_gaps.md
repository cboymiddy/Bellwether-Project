# summer_olympics gaps and disagreements

Primary = Wikipedia per-edition infobox (Opening/Closing). Second = Olympedia edition pages.
Disagreements are reported, not resolved; table values follow Wikipedia.
Olympedia host country names differ in style from Wikipedia (e.g. 'Great Britain' vs 'United Kingdom'); host_country is the Wikipedia string, olympedia_host keeps Olympedia's.
Olympedia ceremony columns are blank where Olympedia gives none (1900: it lists no ceremonies; 1904: no closing ceremony). Wikipedia's 1900 introduction says there were no ceremonies at the start or end of the Games (the page tags that statement 'citation needed'); the infobox 1900 dates are therefore start/end of the event programme, not ceremonies.
1904 spans 1 July to 23 Nov (Wikipedia) / 26 Nov (Olympedia competition end), straddling Election Day 1904-11-08 under both sources. Olympedia also lists a 14 May 1904 opening ceremony.
