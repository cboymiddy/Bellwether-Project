- Ordering: Wikipedia (Preakness and Belmont pages) report that the order of the three races was not always Derby, Preakness, Belmont: the Preakness came first eleven times before 1931/1932, the Derby and Preakness shared a day on 1917-05-12 and 1922-05-13, and the Belmont came before the Preakness eleven times; since 1931 the order is fixed. triple_crown_won here only requires the same horse to win all three in the calendar year, regardless of order.
- 1918: Wikipedia lists two Preakness winners (War Cloud and Jack Hare Jr., two divisions) and one Belmont winner (Johren); both Preakness names are joined with ' / ' in preakness_winner.
- Belmont per-year Wikipedia pages exist only for 1920, 1948, 2000, 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024 among election years; all other Belmont dates are unsourced.
- Follow-up search: Wikipedia articles for each winner were fetched (raw/horse_pages); only 1932 and 1984 state a usable date. The 1900 Ildrim article cites a New York Times article dated 25 May 1900 (a publication date, not stated as the race date), so 1900 is left empty. Daily Racing Form archive (drf.uky.edu) and the Wikipedia top-three-finishers page were not fetchable. Preakness dates were not pursued.
